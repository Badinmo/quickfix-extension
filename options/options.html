Kalam only ever reads the text you have selected, or the single input/textarea you are focused in, at the moment you trigger it. It does not read the rest of the page, other tabs, or anything else. No middleman: your text goes straight from your browser to your own AI account. We never see it. Treat it the way you would treat pasting it into any other external tool.
